Lifecycle Model

The policy architecture uses 000 Master MacOS Policy as the common configuration baseline. 001 Pre-Enrollment Policy and 002 Post-Enrollment Policy apply lifecycle-specific overrides where a configuration should be active only during one stage of enrollment or steady-state operation.

A setting shown as disabled in a child policy is treated as an intentional override of the overall architecture rather than evidence that the component does not exist.
