Overview

Description

Compound Conditions allow multiple individual conditions or criteria to be combined into a larger logical condition for more precise device targeting, monitoring, health-state evaluation, notification behavior, or automation triggering.

Unlike the standard Condition documented in Topic I, a Compound Condition can evaluate multiple underlying condition states together before determining whether the overall condition is considered met.

Compound Conditions are not currently used within the macOS policy architecture.

This section is retained in the documentation so that the absence of Compound Conditions is explicitly recorded rather than leaving the feature undocumented.

Potential Function

Compound Conditions may be used in the future if a workflow requires multiple device-state conditions to be evaluated together before an action or monitoring state should apply.

Examples could include requiring multiple enrollment, application, service, hardware, or compliance conditions to simultaneously meet specified criteria before triggering a resulting condition state or automation.

Any future Compound Conditions added to these policies should be documented here individually, including:

- Compound Condition name

- Policies where the condition is enabled

- Underlying conditions

- Logical relationship between those conditions

- Health status

- Severity

- Priority

- Notification behavior

- Associated automations

- Auto-reset behavior
